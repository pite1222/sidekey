SideKey

A Japanese flick keyboard for iPhone. Keys sit on one side; conversion candidates (or your clipboard history when you are not typing) line up vertically on the other. Everything stays on your device — the app makes no network connections. 7-day free trial, then a one-time 490 JPY purchase.
